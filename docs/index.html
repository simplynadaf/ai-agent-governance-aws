How it works

What Traccia governs

🚫 Enforce
A prompt injection is hard-blocked before any model call. The trace proves it: zero sub-agent spans on the blocked run.
guardrail.triggered · enforcement=block
🙈 Protect
Applicant email, phone, and SSN are redacted to [REDACTED_*] on every span in the multi-agent tree, before export.
redact_pii=true
📜 Prove
EU AI Act risk tier + Annex III 5(b) + Art. 50 transparency stamped on every span, sealed with an integrity hash.
eu_ai_act.risk_tier=high · disclosure()
👤 Oversee
Borderline decisions are flagged for a human (EU AI Act Art. 14) instead of auto-deciding.
governance.needs_human_review
⚖️ Be fair
A fairness guardrail blocks any decision that used a protected attribute. Scoring uses income and debt only.
fairness_check · block
🔒 @govern
On the platform, Spend Cap / Model Boundary / Loop Cap can hard-block a runaway crew with an AgentBlockedError.
@govern(fail_open=False)
